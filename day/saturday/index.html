Saturday's four lucky colors

Saturday belongs to Saturn, the planet of patience and lasting results. Saturn's colors are royal purple, midnight navy, deep plum and charcoal. We rotate through them, one per week, so every Saturday has its own shade but always stays in Saturn's family.

What to wear on Saturday for good luck

You don't need a whole outfit. One clear touch of the color is enough: a purple or navy outfit, a dark plum nail color or a charcoal coat. Moon Silver is Saturday's partner color, so it is a safe match if you want to wear two colors.

What Saturday is good for

Saturn's energy is patience, focus and lasting results. That makes Saturday a good day for planning, cleaning up, saving money and work that needs patience. Try to avoid rushing.

Lucky number 8

8 is Saturn's number. Use it for small choices on Saturday: a seat, a locker, the time you send an important message (for example at 8:08), or the number of minutes you spend on a short break.

Signs that feel lucky on Saturday

Saturday is the power day of Capricorn and Aquarius, because Saturn rules both signs. On Saturdays the color of the day is doubly lucky for them.
