Questions

How Lucky Color Today works

How do you choose the lucky color of the day?

Each day of the week is ruled by one of the seven classical planets, a tradition shared by Vedic astrology, Thai color customs and the names of the weekdays themselves. Each planet has its own colors and number. We rotate through that planet's shades week by week, so the same weekday never feels stale.

Is my sign's color different from the day's color?

Yes. Your sign's color blends the day's planet with your sign's ruling planet and element. When the day belongs to your ruling planet, the day color is doubly lucky for you.

What's the difference between my daily color and my birthday color?

Your birthday color comes from your life path number and never changes. Think of it as your signature color. Your daily color is the extra boost for one day.

What time does the new color appear?

The site updates at midnight on your own device's clock. Our Instagram Stories share it with you every day too.

Is this scientific?

No. Lucky colors, horoscopes and palm reading are traditions for reflection and fun, not predictions or advice. Enjoy them, and make your real decisions with care.
