10 easy ways to use your lucky color

November 8, 2026 · Lucky Color Today

You don't need a purple suit on a Saturday. Lucky colors work just as well in small doses.

  1. Socks. Close to your skin and nobody needs to know.
  2. Nail polish. One accent nail is enough.
  3. Hair tie or clip. Easy to swap every morning.
  4. Phone wallpaper. You look at it more than anything else.
  5. A card in your wallet. A square of colored paper next to your money.
  6. Pen or notebook. Especially on exam and meeting days.
  7. Your drink. Green tea on Wednesday, orange juice on Sunday.
  8. A flower on your desk. Cheap, pretty and very visible.
  9. Your screen theme. Change your laptop's accent color.
  10. A bead bracelet. Keep seven, one for each day.
